Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Paw Matcher ("we", "us", or "our") collects and processes personal data when you use our website at https://www.pawmatcher.app and our web application at https://swipe.pawmatcher.app (together, the "Services").

We process personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR where applicable, and other applicable European data protection laws.

1. Data controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is Paw Matcher. For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at info@pawmatchers.app.

2. Personal data we collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Account and profile data: name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), profile preferences, and adoption-related preferences you provide in the app.
  • Usage and interaction data: swipes, matches, favourites, in-app activity, and messages you send to shelters or adoption partners through the platform.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and log data generated when you access the Services.
  • Communication data: information you send when you contact us by email or through support channels.
  • Cookie and similar technology data: as described in Section 9 below.

We do not intentionally collect special categories of personal data (such as health data) unless you voluntarily provide such information in communications with shelters or in your profile. Please only share information that is necessary for adoption purposes.

3. How we use your data and legal bases

We use personal data only where we have a valid legal basis under the GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): to create and manage your account, provide matching features, and facilitate communication with adoption partners.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): to improve and secure the Services, prevent fraud and abuse, analyse aggregated usage, and communicate about important service updates — balanced against your rights and freedoms.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): for optional marketing communications and non-essential cookies where required. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): where we must comply with applicable law, regulatory requests, or court orders.

4. Automated decision-making and profiling

Our matching features may use automated processing to suggest dogs that may fit your stated preferences and lifestyle. This profiling is intended to improve your experience and does not produce legal or similarly significant effects on you without human involvement in the adoption process itself.

You have the right to object to profiling used for direct marketing and, in certain cases, to request human review of automated decisions that significantly affect you. Contact us at info@pawmatchers.app to exercise this right.

5. Who we share data with

We may share personal data with:

  • Adoption partners and shelters when you express interest in a dog or initiate contact through the app.
  • Service providers (processors) who help us host, operate, analyse, or support the Services (e.g. cloud hosting, email delivery, analytics), under data processing agreements that meet GDPR requirements.
  • Authorities where required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. International transfers

Your data is primarily processed within the European Economic Area (EEA). If we transfer personal data outside the EEA or UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or an adequacy decision, unless another valid transfer mechanism applies.

7. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • After account deletion, we delete or anonymise personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate legal claims.
  • Technical logs are typically retained for a limited period for security purposes.

8. Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the EEA, UK, or another jurisdiction with similar rights, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data and receive a copy (Art. 15)
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data (Art. 16)
  • Erase your data in certain circumstances (Art. 17)
  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances (Art. 18)
  • Data portability for data you provided (Art. 20)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. A list of EU authorities is available at edpb.europa.eu.

To exercise your rights, email info@pawmatchers.app. We will respond within one month, as required by the GDPR, and may request information to verify your identity.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for the Services to function (e.g. authentication and security). Where we use non-essential cookies (such as analytics or marketing), we will request your consent before placing them, in line with the ePrivacy Directive and applicable national laws.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings and, where available, through our cookie consent tool in the app. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the Services work.

10. Children

The Services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

11. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Where changes are material, we will provide additional notice where required by law.

13. Contact

For privacy enquiries or to exercise your data protection rights, contact us at info@pawmatchers.app.
